Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial? ▾
Yes! All plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You can explore all features before committing.
Can I import my existing clients? ▾
Absolutely. You can import clients via CSV, or add them one by one. We also support migration from other coaching platforms.
How does the AI plan generator work? ▾
CoachOS AI analyzes each client's profile — goals, medical conditions, macros, check-in history — and generates personalized meal and workout plans instantly.
Is my data secure? ▾
Yes. We use enterprise-grade encryption (AES-256) and store all data in secure, SOC-2 compliant data centers. Your client data is never shared.
Can my clients access their plans? ▾
Yes! Clients get their own portal where they can view assigned plans, track habits, and see their progress. Available on Pro and Elite plans.
Do you offer refunds? ▾
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If CoachOS isn't right for you, we'll refund your payment — no questions asked.
